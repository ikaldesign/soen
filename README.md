Tone: Calm, meditative, timeless. Feels like still water or reflection.

Why it fits: Embodies the “be like water” philosophy literally. Philosophical, spiritual undertones without feeling heavy-handed.
Visual Direction:

Minimal sans-serif logo, subtle brush-like curves.

Subnames:

Mood: reflective simplicity — like ink on still water

Kaia

Tone: Organic, grounded, modern. Gentle strength.
Meaning / Origin: Hawaiian for “sea”; also from Scandinavian roots meaning “pure” or “earth.”
Why it fits: Balances nature and structure — exactly like your framework philosophy: fluid but foundational.
Visual Direction:

Rounded geometric typography, wave-inspired curves.

Soft teals, sandy neutrals, and minimal gradient light.
Subnames:

Kaia Core, Kaia Playground, Kaia Flow, Kaia Modules.
